# Rule: Discretion, Trademarks & Professionalism

1. **Strictly Avoid Proprietary Trademarks:**
   - Do NOT reference commercial trademarks, titles, publishers, or official servers by name.
   - Use discreet and neutral terminology: "classic 2.5D MMORPG", "heritage Pre-Renewal mechanics", "classic isometric combat", "legacy .gat format", "reference emulator models".

2. **Professional & Non-Adversarial Tone:**
   - Maintain a neutral, constructive, engineering-focused tone at all times.
   - Never write offensive, derisive, or hostile statements regarding any company, game studio, or online service.

3. **Clean-Room Engineering:**
   - Project Hades is an original, clean-room MMORPG backend architecture written in Rust with modern protocols (WebTransport/QUIC) and zero-allocation tick loops.
